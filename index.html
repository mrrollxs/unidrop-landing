One App, Three Ways to Share

UniDrop integrates three powerful tools into a single, clean, and functional interface.

Share Anywhere, with a Link.

Send files to any device, anywhere in the world, with a simple web link valid for 24 hours. Lost the link? No problem. You'll find all your generated links from the last 24 hours in your history.

Effortless Nearby Sharing.

Send a photo, video, or document to any nearby device in a flash. Forget operating system and network barriers and share with ultimate simplicity.

Your Clipboard, On Command.

Pair your devices and use the quick sync button to send copied text from one screen to another. Simple and fast.
